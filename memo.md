## Key Insight
Between April and May 2026 [HIGH], Guntur experienced an exceptional **+122.19% surge in sales** [HIGH] (an absolute increase of **+₹76,296.66** [HIGH], rising from ₹62,442.27 [HIGH] to **₹138,738.93** [HIGH]). 

This expansion was driven by a powerful dual-engine mechanism [MEDIUM]:
1. **Order Volume Expansion**: Total orders rose from 51 to 77 [HIGH] (**+50.98%** [HIGH]), with total units sold increasing from 139 to 232 [HIGH] (**+66.91%** [HIGH]).
2. **Basket Value (AOV) Inflation**: Average Order Value expanded by **+47.16%** [HIGH] (from ₹1,224.36 to **₹1,801.80** [HIGH]).

Crucially, this growth was not evenly distributed across categories [MEDIUM]. A full **74.79% (₹57,063.48)** [HIGH] of the entire ₹76,296.66 monthly revenue gain [HIGH] was concentrated in just two premium, high-unit-value product lines [MEDIUM]: **Wellness & Nutrition (+175.09%)** [HIGH] and **Medical Devices (+245.25%)** [HIGH].
